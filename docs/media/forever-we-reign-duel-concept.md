## Translation into the original game direction

The image reads strongly as grounded medieval plate-and-cloth fantasy. Treat
its weathered ivory armor as a color and wear reference for the game's ceramic
plates, then author explicit ceramic thickness, repairs, brass fasteners, and
service cloth in 3D. Do not infer material composition from pixels alone.

The approved setting also needs its own weather-engine identity: functional
brass storm channels, repaired ceramic gate plates, restrained luminous glass,
and maintained coastal machinery. The concept suggests some of these through
warm metal details and blue architectural glazing, but the large fortress should
be adapted into the existing foundry and observatory kits. The crown-and-tree
banner design is a reference motif, not an approved faction emblem or new canon.
Names and detailed curse lore remain proposals in the [lore bible](../design/forever-we-reign-lore-bible.md).

Keep the throne and explicit curse outside the early courtyard encounter. The
ending reveal retains its existing campaign boundary. The [production brief](../production/realistic-art-production-brief.md)
defines the next bounded asset work and the later finished arena recipe.
